Study Abroad

🇲🇾 Study in Malaysia

Affordable tuition and living costs, English-medium degrees, and a Muslim-friendly environment — halal food everywhere, mosques on campus, and one of the largest international Muslim student communities in Asia.

Major universities

Universiti Malaya (UM) · International Islamic University Malaysia (IIUM) · Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia (UKM) · Universiti Sains Malaysia (USM) · Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM) · Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM)

Admissions & visa: apply to the university first. Once you hold an offer, your student pass is processed through EMGS (Education Malaysia Global Services) — the official channel; never pay an unofficial agent for it.

EMGS — Education Malaysia ↗

Mufti is based in Malaysia himself — use the Ask Mufti guidance form in this hub and ask him directly.

🇹🇷 Study in Turkey

Türkiye Scholarships (Türkiye Burslari) — the Turkish government's fully funded scholarship programme: tuition, a monthly stipend, accommodation, airfare and a free one-year Turkish language course are covered under it. Applications are free, and only through the official portal:

Türkiye Scholarships — official portal ↗

TR-YÖS: the central entrance examination for international students applying to Turkish universities — many self-funded admissions run through it.

Language: most degree programmes are taught in Turkish. A TÖMER certificate is the standard proof of Turkish proficiency — and the scholarship's free language year counts towards it.
